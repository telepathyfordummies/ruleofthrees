He enters quietly, always unnoticed,

A shadow weaving through the corners closest.

Twisting the threads that fate had untucked,

His first step is guided by luck.

Three.

The cipher shifts each time you invoke his name.

Now is the time to question the order you thought would remain.

A subtle turn, a shift unstable.

He has been here all along whispering.

You just didn't hear him yet.
